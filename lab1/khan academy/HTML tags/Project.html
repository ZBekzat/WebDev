Spaghetti Bolognese

  • Time: 40 minutes
  • Serves: 4
Ingredients Quantity
Spaghetti 400g
Beef mince 500g

Step 1: Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions.

Step 2: Fry the beef until browned and add tomato sauce.

Step 3: Combine spaghetti with sauce and serve hot.

Source: BBC Good Food

Grilled Chicken Salad

  • Time: 30 minutes
  • Serves: 2
Ingredients Quantity
Chicken breast 2 pieces
Lettuce 100g

Step 1: Grill the chicken until fully cooked.

Step 2: Chop the lettuce and vegetables.

Step 3: Mix the grilled chicken with salad and drizzle with dressing.

Source: AllRecipes

Vegetable Stir Fry

  • Time: 25 minutes
  • Serves: 3
Ingredients Quantity
Broccoli 200g
Bell peppers 2 pieces

Step 1: Chop all vegetables into bite-size pieces.

Step 2: Heat oil in a pan and stir-fry vegetables for 5-7 minutes.

Step 3: Add soy sauce and cook for another 2 minutes. Serve hot.

Source: Homemade Recipe
